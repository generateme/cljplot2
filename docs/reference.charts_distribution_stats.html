18.1 histogram

Bars counting the values falling into each bin; groups overlay, dodge, stack or mirror, and a categorical :y gives one histogram per level (ridgelines).

Option Type Default Description
:x (required) a keyword, a function or a categorical marker of one values to bin (data units)
:y a keyword, a function or a categorical marker of one or nil ridgeline levels: categorical or on a :bands scale, one histogram per level (a numeric :y otherwise is ignored)
:color a keyword, a function or a categorical marker of one or nil groups, each with its own bar fill colour
:pattern a keyword, a function or a categorical marker of one or nil groups, each with its own fill pattern
:group a keyword, a function or a categorical marker of one or nil groups without a visual aesthetic 
(plot/render-image {:data iris
                    :mapping {:x :petal-length :color :species}
                    :charts [(chart/histogram)]
                    :labels {:x "petal length [cm]"}}
                   {:width 600 :height 350})

18.1.1 Bins

The bins (a method, a count or breaks), what a bar’s height measures and the space the bins are made in:

Option Type Default Description
:bins keyword or vector of a finite number (at least 2) or quantile bins {:quantiles q :estimation e}: q a positive count or a vector of increasing probabilities strictly between 0 and 1, e one of [:legacy :r1 :r2 :r3 :r4 :r5 :r6 :r7 :r8 :r9] or a positive integer :sturges bins (per panel under free or sliced facet x scales): an estimation method (:sturges :sqrt :rice :doane :scott :freedman-diaconis), a bin count or increasing breaks (shared, values outside dropped)
:y one of :cumulative :percent :count :pmf :density :percent bar height: :percent (of the panel's or group's total), :count, :pmf (shares summing to 1), :density (area 1) or :cumulative (count of the bin and every bin before it)
:stat-space one of :data :scale or nil :data space the bins are made in: :data (data units) or :scale (the x scale's coordinates - equal-width log10 bins on a :log axis, values it can't place dropped; :density per transformed unit) 
(plot/render-image (plot/arrange [{:data faithful
                                   :mapping {:x :eruptions}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins 30 :y :count :stroke-color :none})]
                                   :labels {:title ":bins 30 :y :count"}}
                                  {:data faithful
                                   :mapping {:x :eruptions}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins [1.5 2 2.5 3 3.5 4 4.5 5 5.5] :y :density
                                                              :color [120 160 90] :stroke-color :white})]
                                   :labels {:title "breaks, :y :density"}}
                                  {:data two-species
                                   :mapping {:x :sepal-length :color :species}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:position :mirror :bins 12 :zero-line-color :red
                                                              :zero-line-width 2})]
                                   :labels {:title ":position :mirror"}
                                   :legend false}]
                                 {:columns 3})
                   {:width 1000 :height 320})

What a bar’s height measures - the same bins as shares summing to 1 (:pmf, the default :percent scaled to 100), and the running count (:cumulative):

(plot/render-image (plot/arrange [{:data faithful
                                   :mapping {:x :eruptions}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins 20 :y :pmf})]
                                   :labels {:title ":y :pmf"}}
                                  {:data faithful
                                   :mapping {:x :eruptions}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins 20 :y :cumulative})]
                                   :labels {:title ":y :cumulative"}}]
                                 {:columns 2})
                   {:width 900 :height 300})

On a log axis bins of equal width in data units crowd into the last decades; :stat-space :scale makes them in the axis’s log10 coordinates - equal-width on screen, :density per log10 unit (values a log axis can’t place, 0 and negatives, are dropped and counted). In either space the values are numbers: histogram and density take no dates.

(plot/render-image (plot/arrange [{:data skewed
                                   :mapping {:x :value}
                                   :scales {:x {:kind :log}}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins 20 :y :count :stat-space :data})]
                                   :labels {:title ":stat-space :data"}}
                                  {:data skewed
                                   :mapping {:x :value}
                                   :scales {:x {:kind :log}}
                                   :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins 20 :y :count :stat-space :scale})]
                                   :labels {:title ":stat-space :scale"}}]
                                 {:columns 2})
                   {:width 900 :height 300})

Bins are shared by every facet panel, so panels compare bar by bar. Under a free (or sliced) facet x scale each panel gets bins over its own range instead - a count of bins (here 8) or a method applies per panel, so the bars fill each panel’s own axis; explicit breaks stay shared. Margin histograms follow their panel’s scale the same way.

(plot/render-image {:data iris
                    :mapping {:x :petal-length :facet :species}
                    :facets {:scales {:x :free}}
                    :charts [(chart/histogram {:bins 8 :y :count})]
                    :labels {:x "petal length [cm]"}}
                   {:width 900 :height 300})

18.1.2 Invalid values

Rows whose x is missing, NaN or not a number (or a value the stat space can’t place) are dropped and counted; :invalid :show draws their count as a bar in a band beside the x axis’s start, outside the axis line, on the same y scale, in the chart’s own fill (or the mapped one), labelled by :invalid-label (which needs :invalid :show). Shares (:percent, :pmf) are then of all rows, the invalid ones included; groups stack or dodge in the band as in the bins, every facet panel has its own band and a flipped plot puts it under the panel. Only the main charts of a generated grid draw a band: :invalid :show in a margin chart or a :custom-grid plot is an error.

Option Type Default Description
:invalid one of :drop :show or nil :drop rows with a missing, NaN, non-numeric or unplaceable x: :drop (counted in :dropped) or :show (a bar of their count in a band beside the x axis's start)
:invalid-label string or nil "NA" label of the band of invalid values (needs :invalid :show)
